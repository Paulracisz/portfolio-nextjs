In the RV industry, nobody is pushing innovation harder than Firefly Integrations.

Established in early 2000, the company was founded upon a simple yet powerful idea. innovation, imagination, and integration. 

By combining third party systems, Firefly has created an all-in-one system that works together to provide luxury, convenience and the sophistication of smart home technology to recreational vehicles all around the world. Here's how.

## The CAN BUS system

CAN BUS which stands for controller area network bus, is a effcient communication protocol, mostly designed for use in vehicles. The purpose of CAN bus was to reduce the complexity and cost of electrical wiring in automobiles used in multiplexing. Mutliplexing is a method by which multiple analog or digital signals are combined into one signal over a shared medium. In the case of Firefly, the "multiplex" screen is the touchscreen interface, that uses a logic controller to recieve inputs from various devices. How this works is simple. Since CAN bus has a protocol specifically for RVs called RVC, all manufacturers of third party devices that are meant to go into RVs, such as a solar power controller, must use the RVC protocol when they send out CAN messages. These messages can be transmitted to the logic controller to handle all kinds of digital or analog signals and tell the screen what is currently happening with each piece of hardware. The end result- a touchscreen that can tell you the temperature of the coach, whether or not you have enough power to run your air conditioner, your solar battery percentage level, and which lights you have on, all at once. 


## The Latest Demo, the Firefly Trailer
Firefly has a trailer that they recently built to take to RV shows. The purpose of this trailer is to show RV enthusiasts as well as RV manufacturers, the power of the Firefly system in action. The trailer is equipped with many different devices that demonstrate the flexibility of the system, being able to interface with many different types of hardware, as well as showing off the clean, user friendly interface in both the mobile app and the wall mounted touchscreen.
