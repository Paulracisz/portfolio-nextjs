For my honeymoon in 2021, I traveled to Mexico.

(I translated this article into Spanish with my very basic level of spanish learning).

Para mi luna de miel, fuimos a México.

## Where We Went/ Adónde Fuimos

We stayed in Playa Del Carmen, close to the coast of the Gulf of Mexico in the Yucatán Peninsula. It was very sunny, the weather was beautiful, and the water was a bright bluish green color!

Nos quedamos en Playa Del Carmen. Tiempo hace muy sol, es fue hermosa. Las aguas fue ligero azul.

## The Chichén Itzá

We went to see the Mayan Pyramid, the Chichén Itzá. It is one of the famous wonders of the ancient world. We learned about the brutal Mayan empire. 

Fuimos a ver la Pirámide Maya, el Chichén Itzá. Es un monumento muy famoso. Nos aprendí sobre las ciudades de Mayans.

## Swimming with Dolphins/ Nadar con Delfines

With swam with Dolphins. They did many tricks for their trainers in order to recieve yummy fish. I posed with a dolphin and they took a nice picture.

Nos nadamos con delfines. Hicieron muchos trucos para conseguir un pescado delicioso. Nos tomamos una foto con ellos.
